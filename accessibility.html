Comfort and access

A clearer trail for more travelers.

Accessibility is part of JollyRoute’s product design and testing, not an afterthought.

Current design goals

  • Readable contrast and protective surfaces behind text and controls.
  • Support for larger text, screen readers, keyboard navigation, and meaningful labels.
  • Reduced-motion controls for decorative and transitional movement.
  • Solid, glass, and higher-opacity surface choices.
  • Alternatives to color-only meaning and generous touch targets.
  • Inclusive predefined avatars, including visible mobility aids.

Important limitation

JollyRoute does not certify that a destination, venue, route, transportation option, stay, or activity is accessible. Users should verify accessibility details directly with authoritative providers.

Tell us what blocks you

Email jollyroute.support@gmail.com with the device, screen, task you were trying to complete, and the barrier you encountered. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information first.
