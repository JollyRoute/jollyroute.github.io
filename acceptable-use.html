Acceptable use

JollyRoute is for lawful, consensual vacation planning among invited adults.

Not allowed

Do not use JollyRoute for illegal activity; child exploitation or endangerment; violence or self-harm threats; terrorism; hate; harassment; stalking; bullying; impersonation; defamation; fraud; nonconsensual privacy or location disclosure; sexual exploitation; infringing content; spam; malware; security bypass; scraping; quota abuse; surveillance; emergency operations; high-risk dispatch; or commercial tracking.

Private links still require care

Invitation-only access is not permission to harm others. Replace a leaked invitation, leave or remove participants where safe, and preserve evidence outside the app when appropriate.

Reporting

For a safety or abuse concern, email jollyroute.support@gmail.com. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Remote collaboration status

Production remote collaboration remains disabled until reporting, blocking, filtering, timely moderation, removal, appeals, child-safety escalation, intellectual-property complaints, lawful-request handling, retention, deletion/export, and reviewer access are operational.
