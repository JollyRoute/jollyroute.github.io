Responsible reporting

Security contact

If you believe you found a security issue affecting JollyRoute, please report it privately.

How to report

Email jollyroute.support@gmail.com with a concise description, affected app version and device platform, reproducible steps, and the potential impact.

Protect people and data

  • Do not access, alter, retain, or share another person’s information.
  • Do not perform denial-of-service, social engineering, spam, physical intrusion, or destructive testing.
  • Use test data and the minimum activity needed to demonstrate the issue.
  • Do not publish details while the issue is being assessed.

What not to email

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, signing keys, government identifiers, payment data, precise location history, or unrelated personal information. A formal vulnerability disclosure policy and safe-harbor language will be published before production release.
