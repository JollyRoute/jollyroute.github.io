A little route, made together
Pre-release pilot: JollyRoute is currently being prepared for a United States pilot and is not yet offered as a production service. Policy pages are drafts until marked effective.

Private by design

The current build is local-first. It has no production accounts, backend sync, advertising, analytics, tracking, remote crash upload, remote AI, or enabled production place search.

Made for leisure

JollyRoute is for consensual vacation and leisure planning among invited adults—not navigation, emergency response, monitoring, dispatch, or professional advice.

Verify the trail

Travel details can change. Travelers should independently verify routes, bookings, weather, accessibility, dietary needs, official advisories, and safety information.
