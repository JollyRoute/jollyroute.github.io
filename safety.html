Travel responsibility

Make the plan. Verify the journey.

JollyRoute helps organize leisure-trip ideas. It does not monitor safety or replace authoritative information.

Not an emergency or tracking service

Plans, reminders, widgets, check-ins, searches, and future AI may be wrong, delayed, stale, incomplete, or unavailable. A check-in does not prove identity, location, movement, arrival, wellbeing, or consent.

Verify important details

Independently verify official advisories, weather, routes, closures, transportation, reservations, tickets, passports and visas, customs, laws, permits, accessibility, allergens, dietary needs, health risks, insurance, emergency numbers, and meeting points. Official government, carrier, venue, park, health-professional, and emergency-service instructions take priority.

Stay attentive

Do not interact with JollyRoute while driving or during any activity requiring attention. Do not infer live aircraft, ship, vehicle, or person location from the app.

Emergency

For immediate danger or medical need, stop using JollyRoute and contact the appropriate local emergency number and trusted people. JollyRoute does not dispatch or monitor help.
