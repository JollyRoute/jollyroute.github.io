JollyRoute support

Questions, accessibility feedback, privacy requests, safety concerns, copyright notices, and security reports can all begin here.

Contact us

Email jollyroute.support@gmail.com.

Include the type of request and a brief description. Do not email passwords, authentication codes, precise location history, government identifiers, payment data, medical records, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Choose a clear subject

  • Support: Help with JollyRoute
  • Accessibility: Accessibility feedback
  • Privacy: Privacy request
  • Safety: Safety or abuse concern
  • Security: Potential security issue
  • Copyright: Copyright notice

Immediate danger

JollyRoute does not monitor trips or dispatch help. For immediate danger or a medical emergency, stop using the app and contact the appropriate local emergency service and trusted people.

Current build

The current pre-release build stores trip information locally on the device. There is no production account or cloud support console from which we can retrieve or reset trip data.
