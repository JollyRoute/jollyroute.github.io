Your local data choices

The current app has no production account or cloud profile. Most choices happen directly on your device.

In the app

  • Use a pseudonymous display name and omit optional preferences.
  • Decline check-ins and optional sanitized crash reports.
  • Reduce or hide widget details and disable reminders.
  • Export a redacted local record.
  • Delete an individual trip or all local app data.

Device controls

Removing the app or clearing its storage may delete local information permanently. Keep your own copy of anything important before doing so.

Privacy request

Because the current build has no production account or backend, support ordinarily cannot locate or delete device-local trip data for you. For a privacy question, email jollyroute.support@gmail.com and do not send unnecessary sensitive information.

Future services

If remote services are enabled, this page and the Privacy Notice will be updated with the applicable access, correction, deletion, appeal, and authorized-agent process before launch.
