Privacy notice

A plain-language description of the current local-first pre-release build.

Draft—not yet effective. This notice will be completed with the provider identity, effective date, final released data flows, applicable U.S. disclosures, and legal review before production release.

What the current app handles

The current build processes on the device: an adult self-attestation; a chosen display name and avatar; trips, votes, preferences, plans, invitations, signals, check-ins, alerts and read state; appearance, accessibility, reminder and widget settings; accepted policy versions and a device-supplied UTC timestamp; and an optional user-approved sanitized crash record.

Local-first storage

Trip state is stored in an authenticated encrypted snapshot, with key protection provided by Android Keystore or iOS Keychain/CryptoKit. Local storage is not a cloud backup. Check-ins contain a category and optional typed place or note—not GPS coordinates. There is no continuous or background location tracking in the current build.

What is not enabled

The default build has no production account system, backend synchronization, remote notifications, advertising, analytics, behavioral tracking, remote crash upload, remote AI, or enabled production place search. It does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Sharing

When a user chooses the operating system’s share feature, selected content goes to the app or recipient the user chooses. That third party’s terms and privacy practices apply.

Retention in the current build

  • Check-ins: 24 hours.
  • Planning signals: 72 hours.
  • Planning preferences: 30 calendar days after the trip.
  • Widget projections: no more than one hour.
  • Approved local crash queue: up to five records, until deleted.
  • Trips and the entire local store: until the user deletes them or removes app data.

Your controls

Users can omit optional content, use a pseudonym, decline check-ins and crash reports, reduce or hide widget content, disable reminders, export a redacted local record, delete trips, and delete all local data.

Future services

Remote collaboration, place search, AI, analytics, advertising, remote crash reporting, and other external processing remain disabled until their exact purposes, data flows, providers, retention, safeguards, choices, and store disclosures are reviewed and published. Pseudonymous data may still be personal data.

Contact

For privacy questions, email jollyroute.support@gmail.com. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information.
